Good to know

Questions, answered.

How much does a bouquet cost? +

Our forever bouquets start at just ₹199. The final price depends on the size, number of flowers and extras you choose — tell us your budget and we'll design around it.

How long does it take? +

Every bouquet is handmade to order, so it's ready in 3–5 days after you approve the design.

Can I choose colours and style? +

Yes — everything is customisable: ribbon colours, wrapping, shape (round, heart, square box, cone or single stem) and little extras like chocolates or a name tag.

Will the flowers really last? +

They're made from satin ribbon, so they don't wilt or fade like fresh flowers. Keep them away from water and direct sunlight and they'll stay beautiful for years.
