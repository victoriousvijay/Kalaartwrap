Questions, answered.

Everything you might want to know before ordering your forever bouquet.

Ordering

What is Kala Art Wrap?+

Kala Art Wrap is a Jaipur studio that hand-makes bouquets from satin ribbon — forever flowers that never wilt. Every bouquet is custom: you pick the colours, the shape and extras like chocolates, a note, a name tag, pearls or fairy lights. Prices start at ₹199 and bouquets are ready in 3–5 days.

How do I order a bouquet?+

Message us on WhatsApp or Instagram, or send an enquiry on our contact page. Tell us the occasion, colours and budget — we'll suggest ideas and confirm the design and price with you.

How much does a bouquet cost?+

Our forever bouquets start at just ₹199. The final price depends on the size, number of flowers and any extras. Tell us your budget and we'll design around it.

Can I customise everything?+

Yes — ribbon colours, wrapping paper, the shape (round, heart, square box, cone or single stem) and little extras like chocolates, a handwritten note, a name tag, pearls or fairy lights.

Can I send a photo of a bouquet I like?+

Of course! Share it with us on WhatsApp or Instagram and we'll make one just for you.

Timing & delivery

How long does it take?+

Every bouquet is handmade to order, so it's usually ready in 3–5 days after you approve the design.

Do you deliver to my city?+

We're based in Jaipur. Message us with your city and we'll let you know the delivery options and any charges before you order.

Can I get it urgently?+

Sometimes! Ask us on WhatsApp — if we can make it in time, we'll tell you honestly.

E-bouquet studio

What is an E-bouquet?+

A digital bouquet you design yourself in our E-bouquet studio — pick the shape, flowers, colours and extras, add a note, and download it as a picture.

Is it free?+

Yes, designing and downloading an E-bouquet is completely free.

Can you make my E-bouquet for real?+

Yes! Tap “Order this design” in the studio. We'll receive your design and make a handmade version — it may differ slightly from the illustration because every piece is made by hand.

Care

Will the flowers really last?+

They're made from satin ribbon, so they don't wilt or fade like fresh flowers.

How do I look after my bouquet?+

Keep it away from water, direct sunlight and damp places, and dust it gently with a soft dry brush.

Your details

What happens to my details when I fill a form?+

We only use them to reply to your enquiry and make your order. We never sell them. Read our Privacy Policy for the full details.

Can I ask you to delete my details?+

Yes — just message us on WhatsApp or Instagram and we'll delete them.
