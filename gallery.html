About these bouquets · परिचय

Satin ribbon bouquets, made one petal at a time.

What are they made of?

Every flower in this gallery is folded by hand from satin ribbon in our Jaipur studio. Because there are no fresh petals, the bouquets never wilt, fade or need water — they stay as beautiful as the day you give them.

Can I get one like these?

Yes. Each bouquet is made to order, so you can copy a design you like or change the colours, the shape (round, heart, square box, cone or single stem) and the extras — chocolates, a name tag, pearls, a note or fairy lights.

How much and how fast?

Bouquets start at ₹199 and are usually ready 3–5 days after you approve the design. Send us a photo from this gallery on WhatsApp or through our contact page, or design your own E-bouquet first.
