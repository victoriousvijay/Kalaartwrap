About us · परिचय

What is Kala Art Wrap?

Kala Art Wrap is a small studio in Jaipur, Rajasthan, that hand-makes bouquets from satin ribbon — “forever flowers” that never wilt, fade or need water. Every bouquet is made to order: you choose the colours, the shape (round, heart, square box, cone or a single stem) and extras such as chocolates, a handwritten note, a name tag, pearls or fairy lights. Prices start at ₹199, and a bouquet is usually ready 3–5 days after you approve the design. You can order on WhatsApp at +91 90243 27497, message @kala_art_wrap on Instagram, send an enquiry through our contact page, or design your own bouquet first in our free E-bouquet studio and download it as a picture.
